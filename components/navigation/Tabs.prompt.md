One-line: switches between sections of a page — services by category, pricing by treatment, locations by region.

```jsx
<Tabs items={['Fertility','Pregnancy','Menopause']} onChange={setSection} />
<Tabs variant="pill" items={['All','Virtual','In clinic']} />
```

Underline tabs are uppercase Gotham Bold with a 3px **yellow** active underline. Pill tabs are sentence case and share Tag's geometry.
