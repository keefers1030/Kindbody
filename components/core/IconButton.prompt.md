One-line: circular icon-only button for carousel arrows, close affordances, and toolbar actions.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="arrow-right" label="Next story" variant="outline" />
<IconButton icon="x" label="Close" variant="ghost" size="sm" />
```

Sizes are 34 / 42 / 52px — all at or above the 44px hit target at md and lg; pad `sm` when it is the only control. `label` is mandatory.
