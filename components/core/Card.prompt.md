One-line: the standard Kindbody surface — white with a soft navy-tinted shadow on cream grounds, or a hairline card on white.

```jsx
<Card variant="elevated" interactive>
  <h3>Egg Freezing</h3>
  <p>Preserve your options with a plan built around your timeline.</p>
</Card>
```

Use `elevated` on cream, `hairline` on white, `cream` for quiet insets, `navy`/`yellow` for full-color feature cards. `interactive` lifts to `--shadow-raised`. Never combine shadow and border.
