One-line: patient testimonials and press quotes — Domaine Medium, curly quotes supplied by the component, attribution in uppercase Gotham Bold.

```jsx
<Quote attribution="-New York Patient">Kindbody has provided me with an experience that made me feel valued.</Quote>
<Quote variant="navy" size="lg" attribution="Fortune">The hottest new employee benefit</Quote>
```

Quote patients verbatim and attribute by city, per the brand's own convention. Do not add quotation marks in the children — the component adds them.
